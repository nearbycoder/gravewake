# The armory

33 weapons: the original Iron, Double and Repeater plus 30 additions. Every entry is available through Collector draws, offers and packs. Open **Armory** to inspect any weapon, change rarity, and enter the practice grounds. Escape leaves practice and restores the previous run.

## Rules

Melee weapons use the fire button or E and have no ammunition or reload. Their damage lands during the strike, after the wind-up. R reloads ranged weapons. Fire/reload upgrades also improve melee swing recovery.

Burn lasts three seconds and deals 18 damage/second. Frost slows movement to 35% for three seconds. Poison stacks duration up to six seconds and deals 12 damage/second. Drain restores 12% of direct damage, capped at full health. Piercing loses damage per target; chains weaken at each jump. Grenades and venom orbs arc under gravity and detonate on contact or expiry. Gatling fire accelerates while held. Blackthorn fires three separately timed rounds per burst.

Models, stats and mechanisms are generated from `src/weapons.rs`; rarity is a modifier, not additional weapons counted toward the 33.
